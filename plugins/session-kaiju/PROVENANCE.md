# Provenance

- Original project: [Jiqize/herdr-kaiju](https://github.com/Jiqize/herdr-kaiju).
- Pinned commit: `ba44d36dafd8a5ba757513253ea1eac2934a67b6`.
- License: MIT; full original notice is preserved in `upstream/LICENSE` and
  embedded in the package HTML.
- `upstream/manifest.js`, `city.js`, `state.js`, `main.js`, `roster.js`,
  `news.js`, `top.js`, `pane.js` and `RECIPE.md` retain
  original geometry, animation intent and data mapping. They are not executed.
- `art-inventory.json` records source paths and original/encoded SHA-256.
  `cwebp -lossless -exact` converts the included day/night raster assets without
  resizing or replacing pixels. The small cover is separately resized and
  quantized from the original lizard. The unmodified original Fusion Pixel font is bundled with
  `upstream/OFL-Fusion-Pixel.txt`; `font-inventory.json` records its source SHA-256.

Browser adaptations translate the native renderer to canvas, map Clawdline's
eight states, and use its SDK for summaries and explicit Session navigation.
Opaque per-opening refs assign decorative monsters. Additional districts keep
every Project reachable beyond the original eight-slot city. The full register
retains every Session and complete Unicode title. Damage progression requires
a supplied working-turn timestamp. Persistent historical statistics, native tabs,
terminal controls, automatic acknowledgments and persistent identity are omitted.

The image importer and shared builder are documented in the Codecraft port.
