# Cost optimization

Use this skill when reviewing infrastructure, API, or subscription spend. Work from invoices, usage records, and owned resource inventories rather than estimates alone.

1. Establish current spend, billing period, owner, unit cost, and utilization for each candidate. Mark unavailable data explicitly.
2. Compare elimination, rightsizing, scheduling, and contract changes against reliability, recovery, and team time. Quantify savings and transition cost separately.
3. Prioritize reversible changes with measured impact. Record the guardrail, verification window, and rollback condition before changing a resource.
4. For usage-priced external APIs, follow the repository's prior-approval and hard-cap policy before calls; do not infer approval from a broad audit request.

Do not present speculative savings as realized savings or cancel an owned service without authorization.
