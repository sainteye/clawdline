# User research

Use this skill to plan interviews, usability sessions, discovery research, or synthesis when the user needs evidence about people's behavior.

1. Define the decision the research should inform, the target participants, and what would change your mind. Choose a method that can answer that question.
2. Write neutral prompts and tasks. Keep observation, participant statement, interpretation, and recommendation separate in notes.
3. Record actual participant count, recruitment limits, session conditions, and contradictory observations. Never substitute example sample sizes for observed evidence.
4. Synthesize patterns with links to observations, identify uncertainty, and recommend the smallest next test or product decision.

Obtain proper access to participant data and preserve privacy; this skill does not authorize outreach or external publication.
