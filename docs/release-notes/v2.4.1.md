# Clawdline 2.4.1

Completion notifications arrive after the final answer finishes, with fixes for Codex task networking and a lighter scheduled-run history.

- **Read the answer when the phone buzzes.** A Session delivery report records its receipt immediately and waits to notify you until a fresh observation shows that same conversation idle. Long answers, unreadable or retained state, and restart recovery cannot release the notification early. Concurrent recovery cannot send it twice.
- **Codex tasks can reach the services they need.** Non-interactive full-permission dispatch explicitly enables network access inside the workspace filesystem sandbox, including the local daemon. Other permission profiles keep their existing policies. Existing processes pick up new launch settings when resumed or relaunched.
- **Open scheduled history in smaller batches.** History starts with ten runs and reveals ten more with each press, including keyboard navigation. Stored history retention is unchanged.

Encrypted carrier read checks also wait for their asynchronous send to finish, making connection regression checks follow the real transmission boundary.
