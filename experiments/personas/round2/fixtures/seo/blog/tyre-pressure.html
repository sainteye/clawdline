How to choose tyre pressure for city riding

By Lea Hartmann · 3 March 2026

The number printed on the tyre sidewall is a maximum, not a recommendation. On cobbles and patched asphalt, most riders are faster and more comfortable a good way below it.

Start from the tyre width

As a starting point for a rider of about 75 kg: 28 mm tyres at 5 to 5.5 bar, 35 mm tyres at 3.5 to 4 bar, and 47 mm tyres at 2.5 to 3 bar. Put about 0.3 bar less in the front than in the rear.

Then adjust by feel

If the bike bounces over cobbles, let some air out. If the tyre squirms in corners or you feel the rim hit an edge, add some. Check the pressure every two weeks: tyres lose air slowly even without a puncture.
