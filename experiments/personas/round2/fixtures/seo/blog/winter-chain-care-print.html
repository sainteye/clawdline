Winter chain care: keep your drivetrain running through the salt season

By Jonas Albrecht · 18 November 2025 · Werkbank Cycles, Speichenweg 12, 10999 Berlin

Every January we replace more chains than in the rest of the year together. Road salt and wet grit turn a chain into sandpaper, and a worn chain then wears out the cassette and chainrings as well. Ten minutes a week keeps most of that away.

Clean it, do not drown it

Wipe the chain with a dry rag while you turn the cranks backwards. If it is very dirty, use a little degreaser on the rag, not a jet of water: a pressure washer pushes water past the seals into the bearings.

Use a wet lube or wax, and wipe off the extra

In winter a wet lube lasts longer between rain rides. Put one drop on each roller, turn the cranks for a minute, and then wipe the outside of the chain. Lube that stays on the outside only collects dirt.

Measure wear once a month

A chain checker costs a few euros. When it shows 0.5 % wear on an 11- or 12-speed chain, or 0.75 % on a chain with fewer gears, change the chain before it damages the cassette.
